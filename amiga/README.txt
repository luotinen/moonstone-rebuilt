Optional: put the images of your own Amiga Moonstone disks here.

Disk 1, named

    Moonstone_Disk1.adf

holds the Amiga intro and ending music. Run Setup.bat (or AmigaMusic_Setup.bat). See README.md, "Amiga music".

All three disks (any names ending in .adf, the one above included) hold the Amiga version's sound effects.
Run Setup.bat (or AmigaSound_Setup.bat), then AmigaSound_On.bat. See README.md, "Amiga sound effects".
Without them the same effects are built from the DOS game's samples and the substitute sounds in the
sounds folder; with them every sound is the Amiga's own.

The images stay on your machine: nothing made from them is part of Moonstone Rebuilt.
