# Moonstone Rebuilt – fixes and improvements

Addresses are in the unpacked programs: `1000:xxxx` is code in `MAIN.EXE`, `DS:xxxx` its data, `INTR` the intro
program. Every patch checks the original bytes first, so it is never applied to a different file.
*Tested* means confirmed in play; *from the code* means worked out from the program and checked in an emulator, but
not yet confirmed in play.

## Bug fixes in the original game

| ID | Fix | Status |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | **A sound that never played.** The sample name table lists `cheap2b` twice (DS:8721, entries 8 and 9); the list is alphabetical, so entry 8 is meant to be `cheap1b`, and CHEAP1B never played. | from the code |
| K2 | **Ratmen and the moon.** The manual says ratmen grow stronger as the moon waxes, but the game made them strong at full moon and strongest at new moon (1000:23FE). Now: strongest at full moon (12 hit points, bite 8, claw 5), strong at ¾ moon (7 / 6 / 3), normal otherwise. | from the code |
| K4 | **Object slots can run out in fights.** Fight objects such as fire, hit effects and daggers need a game slot (20) and an animation slot (only 10). When the animation slots were full, the game slot stayed taken for the rest of the fight (1000:3E89); with all 20 gone, the next object was written past the table (DS:76B0) over the fight's own data. The dragon fight makes the most objects when it is fought from a distance (fire breath, daggers). Now a slot that gets no animation slot is freed at once (1000:3EA5, 10 bytes). In play the limit is hard to reach (a dragon fight with fire and three daggers in the air uses about 8–9 of the 10 animation slots), so this is probably **not** the cause of the dragon fight hang reported on GOG; that cause is still open. | tested: no side effects |
| K5 | **Game Over on the Q key.** On the map, pressing Q ended the game at once, without asking, during any knight's turn (1000:A594). Q is next to W and A, the second player's movement keys, so the game could end by accident: the likely cause of the "unexpected Game Over" reports on GOG. Game Over is now on Esc (one byte). | tested |
| K11 | **Math and Mythral read their stat tables wrongly.** Math's stat gift (1000:B9C2) stepped through its table at the wrong stride: endurance never rose, half of the gifts went to a value that is not a stat, and Math's line was read from a wrong address (in play: a black screen and a long list of the game's internal data). Mythral (1000:BBD5) showed the wrong message, or none, for the stat it changed. Now Math gives strength, endurance or constitution (5/16, 5/16, 6/16) with the matching line, and Mythral the right message. Losing to the Guardian now always costs a real stat point, as intended. | tested |
| K16 | **Duel looting.** When a knight took the loser's weapon and that weapon was the Sword of Sharpness, only the weapon moved, not the sword itself: the loser kept the sword and was armed with it again, so both knights had one (1000:CE91). The sword now moves with the weapon (the code is in `SAVEHOOK.COM`, which needs to be running, as for D6). Also: when a computer knight won and took better armour, the hit points that moved with it came from its own old armour (1000:0B8E); a computer knight's stat raise after a duel win had no limit of 5, unlike a player's (1000:04B0); and when the loser had no lives left, the sword was set as the dead loser's weapon (1000:0C4B, harmless). | from the code |
| K17 | **Ratmen disease removed.** A ratman's bite infected the knight, and every four days (each moon phase) a diseased knight lost a life point until healed, without any message in the game, so players took it for a bug. The bite no longer infects, and a knight infected in an older save no longer loses lives to it. | from the code |
| K18 | **The dragon's hoard grows with its victories, not from game to game.** Every new game (and every practice) added four magic items and gold to the hoard without clearing it, so after several games in one session it held far more than one game's worth. Now it is rolled anew at each new game, and every time the dragon beats a knight it gets one more magic item at a 50 % chance and 0-9 gold (1000:1DBE, 0D23; needs `SAVEHOOK.COM`). `custom_rules.ini`: `dragon_hoard = reset` (no growth) or `keep` (the original, for speedruns). | emulator-tested; not yet tried in play |
| K19 | **Moonstone names.** The item screen called one of the two half-moon Moonstones "New moon Moonstone". The names now follow Stonehenge's check, which decides the win (1000:1053), and the icons: one new-moon, one full-moon and two half-moon stones (1000:C766). | emulator-tested; not yet tried in play |
| K20 | **Moon pictures in the wrong order.** The map showed full, half, three-quarters, crescent, new: the half and three-quarter pictures were swapped (the cycle DS:05A9). They are drawn the other way round now (1000:9053), so the half moon shows on the days the half-moon stones count. The days themselves do not change. | emulator-tested; not yet tried in play |
| K25 | **Instant kills in co-op hit the wrong knight.** The monsters' instant kills (troll, mudman, Balok, dragon...) set the HP of the one-to-one fight's knight to -1 (`1000:0AB2`); in co-op that was the first knight standing, while the knight hit played its death, stood up again and stayed the monster's target. Now the knight the monster fights dies. The knight's handler (`1000:3ED0`) never looked at its HP, so in co-op a knight down now gets the death fall once and stays; the troll looked only at knight 1's HP (`1000:55F9`) and now looks at the knight it fights. A knight down goes off the screen after its fall; a mudman that left with a knight still counted as on the screen, so no other came; now the game's own top-up (`1000:0243`) brings the next. | not yet tried in play A knight down leaves no body behind, and a mudman pulls under (or lets go of) the knight it holds, not another (`1000:9AFB`, `52FE`, `559C`, `54DB`). |
| K26 | **The troll swung at the air.** The troll counted as level with its knight within 5 in depth either way, but its swing hits only when its depth is 0-4 more than the knight's. 1-5 nearer, it stood swinging at the air for as long as the knight stood still - for example a knight at the screen's edge with the troll off the screen. Now it steps until it is level enough to hit (`1000:570E`). It also swung from 91 away on, where its stick passes a standing knight (that needs about 100); now it steps back within 104 (`1000:573B`). |
| K28 | **The Balok froze a knight in co-op.** The Balok's hold pauses the knight it touched (`1000:37A7`) but let go of the knight it fights at that moment (`37FC`, read from `[0978h]`), which with 2-4 knights could be another one: the knight held stayed frozen and the fight stuck. The knight it takes hold of now stays its knight while the hold lasts (`35AF`), and the Balok's frames of the hold show that knight in its own colours (they were always blue). The Balok's bite and squeeze, and the mudman's pull from below, also kill from their scripts, which took the knight nearest the monster: they now kill the knight it holds. The spear trogg's frames of a knight on its spear (`2F90`) show that knight in its own colours. |
| K29 | **The dragon's blind spot.** The dragon always faces right (`1000:3C2B` sets it after `3CFF` has turned it to its knight) and keeps to x 30-100, so a knight at the left edge, behind it, was never attacked while its own blows hurt the dragon. At the dragon's turn (the update hook, `98FD`) the knights of the fight are kept at least 20 (the distance the dragon backs off from, +54h) right of its x, the object and its animation. | from the code |
| K30 | **Monsters' special attacks in the knight's colours.** In co-op the frames of the ratmen (RATMEN2.CEL 42-62), the boar (BE1.C 34-61), the mudmen (MUDMEN1/2.CEL) and the Guardian (DEMON2/3.CEL) that show a knight were drawn in the first knight's colours; they now take the colours of the knight the monster goes for (D15), at the draw hook (`9AFD`, `9B09`). | from the code |
| K31 | **A dagger thrown to the left never left the fight.** The dagger's handler (`1000:3E6F`) ended it only with its x between -10 and 0, and it flies 20 a frame, so most daggers thrown to the left skipped that window and flew on for good. They filled the fight's 20 object slots, and then no dagger could be thrown at all (the Black Knights' throws showed nothing). It now ends at x -10 or less (`3E7D`). | from the code |
| K32 | **The computer knights kept to a few lairs.** Their lair choice sorts the 24 lairs by distance (`1000:A986`), but each swap stored a value in the wrong field (`A9AA`), so only about 4 different lairs were left in the table. Speedrun mode keeps the game's own behaviour. | emulated |
| K33 | **Limits and checks.** The game ends when no human knight can play again (a dead knight and a frog gave a false Game Over; two dead knights with a computer knight between them never ended; `A67B`). HP stay at 1 or more when armour is taken (`CE75`, `0BAD`), and automatic loot moves the HP of the armour actually taken (`0BAD`). A flight with no free path slot lands where it is (`2CFB`). Mythral's table is not read past its end (`BC2E`). Limits are tested with >= instead of = (daggers `CE1A`, `CE26`; gold `CEF8`), and a knight below 0 lives is not fought by the dragon (`0D13`). | emulated |
| K34 | **A lair's monster count overwritten.** A lair fight leaves `[898Dh]` = 2 until the next turn (`B1B0`), and every fight's end (`039E`) stored its own count in the last lair while it was 2, so a dragon attack in the same turn changed that lair. The end of a fight now clears the mark. | tested in Unicorn, not played |
| K27 | **The dragon breathed fire at the air.** The dragon keeps to the left of the screen and took up its far fire only for a knight 140 or more away; its near fire does not reach 96, so a knight 96-139 away was never hurt. The far fire now starts at 96 (`1000:388C`). |
| K24 | **Moonstone damage bonus at the wrong phase.** In its phase a Moonstone doubles the knight's damage, but the new-moon stone did so at half moon and one half-moon stone at new moon, the other way round from Stonehenge and the stones' names and icons (1000:2DC1, 2DE2). | emulator-tested; not yet tried in play |
| K6 | **The dragon and the Guardian could be stun-locked.** Every hit restarted their hit reaction, so quick blows (the upward thrust under the dragon) kept them from ever fighting back. After a hit that counts, further hits do not count for a short time (`dragon_hit_recovery`, `guardian_hit_recovery`; 0.8 s; 1000:3861, 4F6A). | emulator-tested; the time needs a play test |
| K21 | **The dragon's target could be rerolled.** Every time the map was drawn anew (for example after the item screen), the dragon picked a new random target (1000:A78C). It now keeps a target that still has lives; the Scroll of the Wyrm still picks a new one. | emulator-tested; not yet tried in play |
| K22 | **The extra-turn glitch.** After a fight or a visit that used up the turn, the game read the keys again before ending the turn, so Enter pressed at the right moment started another fight or visit the same day (1000:A5B8). | emulator-tested; not yet tried in play |
| K23 | **Lives back after losing to the Guardian.** The Guardian takes three lives and could take them below zero; a healing potion then gave the knight five lives (an unsigned compare, 1000:CCB6). A knight without lives now gets none from a potion. | emulator-tested; not yet tried in play |
| K10 | **Sound effects at the wrong speed.** The DOS sound files are the Amiga version's samples byte for byte, but every DOS file says 16,129 Hz, while the Amiga plays them at 4,766–22,655 Hz. Most sounds played too fast and too high (the dragon's grunts 3.4 times). Each file now carries its real rate (one byte per file; the rates come from the Amiga version, no Amiga files needed). | tested |
| K35 | **Many sounds played the wrong sample.** The DOS code and animation scripts ask for the Amiga version's sound numbers (every sound routine has the Amiga's constants), but the Sound Blaster table (`DS:7E6E`, sound id → sample, 364 entries) was filled in against another numbering, so about 50 of the ids the game uses played another sample although the right one was loaded. Among them: ids 82-109 were one entry off (one rat leap in four played the crush sound, half the mud voices played `STRETCH`, the mudmen screamed like rats); the Guardian's screams played the dragon's muffled cry or the beast's roar; the beast's own roar and the troll's grunts played the default `HIT3`; a knight in the dragon's mouth sounded like a troll; the hit after a Balok's thrash was a sword clash; the troggs' blood, spear and slice sounds were rotated. Three routines asked for the wrong ids: the mudmen's scare voice (`1000:57D8`) for a grunt or the head chop, the troll's grunt (`1000:5801`) for the Balok's roars, and the rat on the skull (`1000:3DC1`) for the impale sound half the time. All now play what the Amiga plays, or the closest DOS sample where DOS has none (rat bite, strain, the beast's gallop). Ids nothing asks for are unchanged. Moonstone Rebuilt's own sounds have ids from 171 up (the mines used id 26). No Amiga files needed. | tested in Unicorn, not listened to |
| K36 | **Every sound at one pitch.** The Amiga plays the same sample at a different rate for each sound (footsteps at three pitches, the Balok's roars at four, the troll's grunts 2.5 times slower than the file's rate, the beast's roar 1.7 times faster); DOS played one rate per file, and K10's rates are the files' own, not the game's. The sound routine now notes the id (`1000:5995`), and before the driver call (`1000:9537`, int 63h) SAVEHOOK.COM writes that id's time constant into the copied sample (table at `DS:7F22`; ids whose DOS sample is a stand-in keep the file's rate). Rates above 22,727 Hz are capped. | tested in Unicorn, not listened to |
| K37 | **Whole samples where the Amiga plays a part.** The dragon's four grunts are one file and its three chomps another; the Amiga plays one grunt or one chomp per sound (ids 20-26, 49-51), DOS played the whole file each time: three chomps for every bite. The K36 handler now plays only the id's part (list at `DS:7DC2`): it writes a sample block header before the part and an end mark after it in the copy of the sample made for that sound. | tested in Unicorn, not listened to |
| K38 | **A blocked blow was silent.** The block check (`1000:4219`) is the Amiga's routine with the call that plays the sword clash (sound 17) left out: it loaded the number and returned. In the original the clash was heard when a knight was struck instead, because the hit's id played the clash sample (K35). The end of the routine is rearranged in its own 14 bytes so that a block that holds plays the clash. The knight's grunt when struck (`1000:3D66`, also switched off in the DOS code) stays off: with one voice it would cut the hit. | tested in Unicorn, not listened to |
