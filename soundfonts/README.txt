General MIDI SoundFonts for the GM music (Music_GM.bat).

GeneralUser-GS.sf2 - GeneralUser GS 2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins, included unchanged under its own
license (GeneralUser-GS-LICENSE.txt, GeneralUser GS License v2.0). It is the SoundFont the GM music uses
unless you put one of your own here. Updates and more of the author's work: https://www.schristiancollins.com
Setup.bat checks that this file is the one the release ships (SHA-256).

To use another General MIDI SoundFont, put it here named

    default.sf2

It is then used instead of GeneralUser-GS.sf2, also for GM music with the Amiga sound effects, where the switches
and Start.bat merge it with the effects into _musicmigamiga_gm.sf2 (made on your machine; do not share it).
